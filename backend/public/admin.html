How the dashboard works

A quick reference for staff using the loan/return dashboard day to day.

Loaning a car

Cars are grouped by team on the dashboard (LSM/LMM, B1 Engineer/Technician, B2 Engineer, Cat A Technician, Cabin Team). Tap "Loan this car" on any available (green) car, enter your staff number, and your name fills in automatically if you're already in the system. If not, you'll be asked to register your name on the spot. Complete the short vehicle inspection checklist (exterior, interior, fuel level - defaults to "Yes" so it's quick, just flip anything that's not OK to "No") and confirm the loan.

Returning a car

Find the car (shown in red / "Loaned Out"), tap "Return this car", and confirm the staff number of whoever is returning it - it doesn't have to be the same person who took it out.

Remarks

Noticed something wrong with a car (dirty, AC not cooling, dead battery, etc.)? Tap "+ Add a remark" on that car's card. It shows as a warning banner on the card for everyone until someone clears it (up to 5 remarks can be open on one car at a time). No login is needed to add or clear a remark.

Shift & team

The header shows which of the 4 rotating teams (A/B/C/D) is currently on duty and whether it's the Day (06:00-18:00) or Night (18:00-06:00) shift - this is calculated automatically and gets attached to every loan record, no manual entry needed.

Admin panel quick reference

Cars tab

Add cars one at a time or bulk-import via CSV. Edit a car to update its details, manage its allocation (which controls which dashboard section it appears in - leave it blank to keep a car hidden from the dashboard until it's ready), and attach documents (Insurance, Mulkiya/Registration, Airport Permit, Other - up to 4 per car). If a car is loaned and the staff member forgot to return it in the app, use the Return button here.

Staff tab

Add staff manually or bulk-import via CSV (staff number in column A, name in column B). Names can be edited any time.

Records tab

Every loan is logged here with the duty team, timestamps, and a link to view the full vehicle inspection checklist that was completed at pickup. Filter by date, team, staff number, or car, and export to CSV any time.

Monthly archives

Past months are archived to a downloadable CSV file and cleared from the live system automatically to keep things fast (this also happens automatically in the background). You can also archive a month manually, or just download a snapshot without deleting anything. All archived files stay listed and downloadable here.
